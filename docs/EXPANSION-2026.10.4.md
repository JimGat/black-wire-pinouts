## What is available

- Official NodeMCU DevKit 0.9 and 1.0 schematics, pinned to source commits, supplement the existing physical maps and instruction PDFs.
- M5Stack hardware guides and board/subassembly schematics are saved alongside their model pages, including ToughC5 and Unit 8Servos2-I2C.
- Physical maps cover ATOM Lite, StampS3A, Nesso N1 connectors, Waveshare ESP32-S3-Tiny N8R8, ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2, and the Core52840 module within nRF52840 EvalKit.
- Nesso N1 originals are preserved at full resolution. Screen previews use a 6000 px maximum edge to avoid decoding 144-megapixel originals during browsing.
- ToughC5 manual pages 8 and 9 are exposed as uncropped connector-function sheets. They are not full-device physical pinouts.
